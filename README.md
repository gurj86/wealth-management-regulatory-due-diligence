# Wealth Management Regulatory Due Diligence Toolkit

A fictional portfolio demonstration showing how regulatory due diligence could be structured for the acquisition of a UK wealth-management / financial-advice business.

## What this demonstrates

- Advice-file review and suitability assurance
- Consumer Duty and customer-outcome assessment
- Ongoing advice-service evidence review
- Pension / retirement-advice risk indicators
- Vulnerability and support-needs considerations
- Acquisition risk identification and escalation
- Potential remediation exposure using transparent illustrative assumptions
- Pre-completion and post-completion action planning
- Executive M&A risk reporting

## Fictional acquisition scenario

A buyer is considering acquiring **Northbridge Financial Planning Ltd**, a fictional advice firm with:

- 3,000 clients
- £750m assets under advice / management
- 60 sampled client files
- wealth, investment and pension business

All firms, customers, figures and findings are fictional and created solely for this demonstration.

## Regulatory references

The toolkit is designed around public FCA material including:

- COBS 9 suitability requirements
- Consumer Duty / PRIN 2A
- FCA review of ongoing financial advice services
- FCA review of consolidation in financial advice and wealth management
- FCA vulnerability guidance

This is a portfolio prototype, not legal or compliance advice, and does not make real acquisition, suitability, redress or regulatory decisions.
